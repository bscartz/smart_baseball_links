Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

This policy explains what information the Smart Baseball app ("Smart Baseball," "we," "us") collects and how it is used. Smart Baseball is an independent app built by one developer.

The short version

  • We collect your account details and the game logs you enter.
  • We don't show ads or use third-party analytics or crash reporting tools.
  • Coaches can see your data only if you choose to connect with them.

Information we collect

Account information. When you sign up with your email and a password, we collect your name, email address, password, age, team name, and handedness.

Game logs. We store the game and performance information you enter in the app, such as contact quality.

We do not use third-party analytics, crash reporting, or advertising tools.

How we use your information

  • To create your account and let you sign in
  • To store and display your game logs
  • To share your data with coaches you choose to connect with

Sharing with coaches

Connecting with a coach is voluntary. If you connect with a coach, that coach can see all of the same data you see in the app.

Where your data is stored

Your account information and game logs are stored in a database hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS).

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email smartbaseballadmin@gmail.com.
